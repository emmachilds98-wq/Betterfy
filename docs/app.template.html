Behaviour

Your library

Betterfy keeps up as you file, so this is only for changes made outside Betterfy — a playlist edited in Spotify itself. It re-reads every playlist and can take a few minutes on a large library. This is not the app update: that is Check for updates, under About below.

Sync across devices

Skips and rejections, tags you've corrected by hand, and which playlists are genre playlists show up on your other signed-in devices too. Nothing else is shared — not your library, not the tags fetched for you — and nothing leaves this device until you link a second one.

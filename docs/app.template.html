Behaviour

Your library

Betterfy keeps up as you file, so this is only for changes made outside Betterfy — a playlist edited in Spotify itself. It re-reads every playlist and can take a few minutes on a large library. This is not the app update: that is ⬇ Check for updates, top right.

Sync across devices

Skips and rejections, tags you've corrected by hand, and which playlists are genre playlists show up on your other signed-in devices too. Nothing else is shared — not your library, not the tags fetched for you — and nothing leaves this device until you link a second one.
